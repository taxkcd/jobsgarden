## What it builds

Kokatta is a software agency in Islamabad, Pakistan that calls itself a Web & AI Innovation Lab. It builds websites and web apps, AI agents and voice receptionists, business automation, SaaS/MVPs and SEO for founders and service businesses in North America, Europe, Australia and the Gulf.

Industry and customers: Software agency serving healthcare & dental, construction & home services, professional & local services, and startups & founders

Products:
- Websites & Web Apps
- AI Solutions (AI chatbots, AI agents, AI voice agents, AI receptionist & auto-responder)
- Business Automation (workflow automation with n8n, CRM automation, booking automation, API integrations)
- SaaS & MVP Development
- SEO & Digital Visibility (technical SEO, local SEO, AI search visibility)
