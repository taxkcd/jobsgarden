## The role

Senior Software Engineer role at Klardaten to own product development, DATEV integrations and automation, working remotely in the EU or Berlin with full-time permanent or contract options.

Responsibilities:
- Turn general product goals into clear requirements, a practical scope and technical decisions.
- Build product features, DATEV integrations and automations across frontend and backend.
- Own testing, deployment and ongoing improvement, including resolving problems in production.
- Find answers, resolve blockers and keep the founders informed. Raise decisions that need discussion early.

Requirements (required):
- Strong production TypeScript experience across frontend and backend.
- Experience independently shipping and maintaining software from a general goal, without detailed specifications, predefined tickets or day-to-day task management.
- Practical experience with APIs, databases, authentication and reliable error handling for integrations and automations.
- Testing, deployment and production debugging skills, and the ability to verify AI-generated code and results.
- Clear written and spoken English for day-to-day collaboration.

Requirements (preferred):
- C#/.NET experience
- DATEV integration experience
- German language skills

Team and product context: Work directly with the founders and the team; founders provide business context and agree on goals and priorities; engineer takes ownership of reaching goals and communicates progress.

Work model, location, authorization: Based in the EU · Remote or Berlin · Full time · Permanent or contract · Start as soon as possible

Compensation: Gross annual salary: €72,000–90,000, depending on experience. Full-time contractors are also welcome.
