## The role

The role is for a backend-leaning full-stack engineer to build and expand the next generation of Creative Agents at ElevenLabs, focusing on backend systems, agent infrastructure, and customer-facing experiences. The engineer will work on production agents, backend reliability, quality monitoring, and end-to-end feature ownership in a small, fast-moving team.

Responsibilities:
- Building production agents and expanding their capabilities through prompts, tool use, orchestration, new models and MCP integrations.
- Designing and operating reliable backend systems for asynchronous creative generation and long-running tasks, with strong performance, observability, scalability and security.
- Developing shared evaluations and live quality monitoring, including model-based assessment of image and video outputs.
- Owning features end to end, from identifying user needs and prototyping to shipping across the stack and supporting them in production, alongside product, design and engineering.

Requirements (required):
- Strong backend engineering skills, experience operating production systems, and sound fundamentals in APIs, cloud infrastructure, storage and asynchronous processing.
- Hands-on experience developing agents, with an understanding of prompts, tools, execution logic, evaluations and the challenges of reliable, secure operation.
- Evidence of independent, end-to-end ownership and confidence working across the stack, including frontend delivery when needed.
- Good product judgement, clear communication and enthusiasm for creative agents.

Requirements (preferred):
- Familiarity with HTTP, WebSockets and asynchronous workers.
- Experience with multimodal generation, creative editors, MCP, or building products in a small, fast-moving team.

Team and product context: Small team shaping the capabilities and architecture of the creative agent product, with ownership from backend systems and agent infrastructure through to customer-facing experience.

Work model, location, authorization: This role is remote and can be executed globally. If preferred, work can be done from offices in London, New York, San Francisco, and Warsaw. #LI-Remote
