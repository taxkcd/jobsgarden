## The role

The role is for a Senior Deployment Engineer specializing in air-gapped LLM deployments on GPU infrastructure, focusing on secure, large-scale software deployments with observability using Grafana and Prometheus. The position requires onsite presence in Riyadh for two weeks per month.

Responsibilities:
- Design and support air-gapped deployment environments for LLM-based systems.
- Deploy and optimize LLM workloads on GPU infrastructure.
- Support large-scale software deployments across secure environments.
- Implement and maintain observability using Grafana and Prometheus.
- Collaborate onsite in Riyadh to assess, deploy, and troubleshoot environment-specific issues.

Requirements (required):
- Expertise in air-gapped deployments.
- Experience with large-scale software deployments.
- Experience deploying LLMs on GPUs.
- Hands-on experience with Grafana and Prometheus.
