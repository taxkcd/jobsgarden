## The role

The AI/ML Engineer role at The Agency Fund involves designing, building, evaluating, deploying, and improving AI/ML systems that support nonprofit partners in East Africa, South Asia, and beyond, focusing on real-world impact through AI-powered tools like AI coaches, copilots, and chatbots.

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, evaluate, deploy, and improve AI/ML systems
- Contribute to tools supported by The Agency Fund like Calibrate
- Document technical decisions and maintain engineering standards
- Provide advice and support to NGOs building generative AI products
- Work closely with behavioral scientists and researchers to translate solutions into reusable tools, playbooks, frameworks and publications
- Open-source non-trivial innovations
- Implement rapid development and deployment cycles
- Stay current with applied AI research and bring relevant advances to the team
- Mentor NGOs on industry best practices, culture, and tooling
- Organize workshops and seminars on lessons and best practices
- Conduct site visits to observe NGO operations and draft technical recommendations
- Foster a culture of proactive problem-solving and initiative-taking

Requirements (required):
- 4+ years of hands-on ML engineering experience building, evaluating and improving at least one end-user facing product powered by deep learning beyond integrating model APIs or contributing to ML infra
- 1–2 years of experience building LLM-powered applications with at least one production-grade agent beyond proof-of-concept
- Strong foundation in math, deep learning, LLMs and modern AI systems
- Fluency with prompting and context engineering for agentic systems
- Very comfortable programming in Python
- Hands-on experience evaluating AI systems with deep understanding of dataset and evaluation design
- Ability to read research papers and quickly test relevant ideas
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to communicate technical concepts clearly to non-engineering audiences
- Care deeply about building AI responsibly and with direct social benefit

Requirements (preferred):
- Experience with multilingual NLP or working with low-resource languages (Swahili, Hindi, local languages in East Africa and South Asia)
- Prior work in global health, international development, or social impact technology
- Familiarity with behavioral science or designing AI for behavior change
- Experience with data annotation pipelines and evaluation methodology for LLMs
- Background in AI safety, alignment, or responsible AI frameworks
- Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and deploying models in production
- Publications at top AI conferences or substantial technical writing

Team and product context: The team is a 22-person, flat, entrepreneurial group combining expertise in software, AI, social psychology, and development economics, embedded inside evidence-based nonprofits across East Africa, South Asia, and beyond, with minimal hierarchy and short feedback loops.

Work model, location, authorization: Remote from anywhere; fully remote and globally distributed; open to candidates based in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia; contractor basis; some timezone overlap with East Africa and South Asia is useful but no mandated core hours.

Compensation: Not competing with big-tech salaries; competitive within mission-driven tech space.
