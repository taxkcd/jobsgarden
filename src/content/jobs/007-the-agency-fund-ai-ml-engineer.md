## The role

The Agency Fund seeks an AI/ML Engineer to design, build, evaluate, deploy, and improve AI/ML systems powering platforms and partner applications that support NGOs in East Africa, South Asia, and beyond. The role involves close collaboration with behavioral scientists, mentoring NGO partners, and contributing to open-source AI evaluation tools.

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, evaluate, deploy, and improve AI/ML systems
- Contribute to tools supported by The Agency Fund like Calibrate
- Document technical decisions and maintain engineering standards
- Provide advice and support to NGOs building generative AI products
- Work closely with behavioral scientists and researchers to identify problems and translate solutions into reusable tools, playbooks, frameworks and publications
- Open-source non-trivial innovations from in-kind work
- Implement rapid development and deployment cycles
- Stay current with applied AI research and bring relevant advances to the team
- Mentor NGOs on industry best practices, culture, and tooling
- Organize workshops and seminars to share lessons and best practices
- Conduct site visits to observe NGO operations and draft technical recommendations
- Foster a culture of proactive problem-solving and initiative-taking

Requirements (required):
- 4+ years of hands-on ML engineering experience building, evaluating, and systematically improving at least one end-user facing product powered by deep learning beyond integrating model APIs or building internal dashboards or contributing to ML infra
- 1–2 years of experience building LLM-powered applications with at least one production-grade agent beyond proof-of-concept
- Strong foundation in math, deep learning, LLMs and modern AI systems
- Hands-on experience evaluating AI systems with deep understanding of dataset and evaluation design
- Fluency with prompting and context engineering for agentic systems
- Very comfortable programming in Python
- Comfortable reading research papers and quickly testing relevant ideas
- Experimental mindset with ability to reason carefully about data, model and evaluation together
- Ability to think from first principles and design practical, scalable solutions
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including communicating technical concepts to non-engineering audiences
- Care about building AI responsibly with direct social benefit

Requirements (preferred):
- Experience with multilingual NLP or working with low-resource languages (e.g. Swahili, Hindi)
- Prior work in global health, international development, or social impact technology
- Familiarity with behavioral science or designing AI for behavior change
- Experience with data annotation pipelines and evaluation methodology for LLMs
- Background in AI safety, alignment, or responsible AI frameworks
- Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, Azure) and deploying models in production
- Publications at top AI conferences or substantial technical writing about your work

Team and product context: The team is a 22-person, flat, entrepreneurial group combining expertise in software, AI, social psychology, and development economics. The role involves embedded work inside evidence-based nonprofits across East Africa, South Asia, and beyond, collaborating closely with behavioral scientists and researchers.

Work model, location, authorization: Remote from anywhere; full time; open to candidates based in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia; fully remote and globally distributed; contractor basis; some timezone overlap with East Africa and South Asia is useful but no mandated core hours.

Compensation: Not competing with big-tech salaries but competitive within mission-driven tech space.
