## The role

The Senior Backend Engineer at DuckDuckGo works on the Backend Functional Team, leading complex projects involving Perl, Go, and Node.js to build performant, testable backend systems for consumer-facing privacy products including AI-assisted search and chat features.

Responsibilities:
- Lead complex backend projects across company-wide objectives
- Write pragmatic, performant, testable code in Perl, Go, and Node.js
- Implement AI-assisted answers integrated into the Search product
- Deliver improvements to Duck.ai AI chat platform
- Implement sophisticated data processing pipelines to maintain search relevancy
- Collaborate remotely with peers and solve product issues impacting privacy solutions
- Mentor other engineers and support their career growth

Requirements (required):
- 7+ years of professional experience, primarily focused on backend systems powering consumer-facing products
- Product acumen to reason about technical tradeoffs in terms of user impact
- Familiarity with AI agent tooling: evaluation frameworks, RAG pipelines and prompt engineering
- Fundamental understanding of complex data structures and algorithms
- Data-driven analytical skills for decision-making and experiment-oriented delivery
- Solid project management abilities
- Excellent written and verbal communication
- Previous experience leading and mentoring experienced engineers

Requirements (preferred):
- none stated

Team and product context: Backend Functional Team working remotely on privacy-focused consumer products including search and AI chat; team culture emphasizes trust, inclusivity, empowered project management, and end-to-end ownership.

Work model, location, authorization: Remote-first team; flexible work arrangement with no core hours; average full-time commitment of 40 hours per week; meetings on camera via video conferencing; travel twice a year for all-hands meetup and team retreat; background check required.

Compensation: Regional pay scale with annual salaries: US $190,500; UK £134,600; Canada C$253,500; Europe/Rest of World €157,350; stock options; US-based employees eligible for company-sponsored health benefits.
