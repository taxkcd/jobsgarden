## The role

This role is for a Software Engineer in the Binance Accelerator Program focused on full-stack development with an AI orientation, primarily working on the KYC system's backend and frontend. It is a remote, full-time internship program designed for early career talent including final-year students and fresh graduates.

Responsibilities:
- Participate in the full-stack development and maintenance of the KYC (Know Your Customer) system, covering both backend services and frontend features
- Assist in drafting and implementing the interface documentation for KYC business modules
- Participate in feature testing and regression for KYC releases, and help troubleshoot issues reported by the QA team
- Support the CS team in resolving and classifying customer issues for system improvement

Requirements (required):
- Computer-related major, bachelor's degree (final-year students and fresh graduates are both welcome)
- Solid Java fundamentals and good understanding of OOP; basic awareness of multithreading and networking concepts
- Some exposure to Spring / Spring Boot (coursework or personal projects are sufficient)
- Familiar with basic MySQL usage
- Basic understanding of common middleware such as Redis, RabbitMQ or Kafka (hands-on experience is a plus, not required)
- Full-stack interest: some frontend foundation (HTML/CSS/JavaScript)
- Able to read and write technical documents in English

Requirements (preferred):
- Knowledge of NoSQL databases is a plus
- Familiarity with React or Vue is preferred
- Good spoken English is an advantage
- Hands-on experience with middleware is a plus

Team and product context: The role is part of the Binance Accelerator Program, a 3-6 month internship program for early career talent to gain experience in the blockchain and digital assets space. The candidate will work on the KYC system and collaborate with QA and Customer Support teams.

Work model, location, authorization: Remote from: Asia; Full time; English language required

Compensation: Competitive salary and company benefits; work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on business team)
