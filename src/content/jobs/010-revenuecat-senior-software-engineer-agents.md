## The role

Senior Software Engineer role at RevenueCat focused on building advanced AI agents that manage subscription monetization for app developers, including orchestration, tool execution, context management, evaluation, and human-in-the-loop controls.

Responsibilities:
- Ship agentic features to thousands of developers
- Shape the foundation for orchestration, tool execution, context management, evaluation, and human-in-the-loop controls
- Scope, prioritize, and drive rollout in partnership with Product team
- Ship meaningful user-facing features, monitor usage and adoption, and improve them over time
- Debug issues across frontend, backend, and data; participate in incident response and on-call rotation
- Mentor other engineers and contribute to architecture discussions
- Engage directly with customers to understand impact of work

Requirements (required):
- 8+ years of experience shipping production systems
- Experience building agents with tool use, structured output, context management, evaluation, orchestration
- Strong backend fundamentals with reliable, observable, and secure systems
- High bar for quality and ability to cut scope while delivering value
- Customer obsession and builder mindset
- Ability to quickly familiarize and improve existing codebases
- Experience shipping meaningful user-facing features and improving them
- High-quality, well-tested code and mentorship skills
- Comfort with ambiguity and self-direction
- Curiosity and staying current on AI tools and practices
- Clear communication in a fully remote team

Requirements (preferred):

- Built Slack integrations
