## The role

The Senior Software Engineer role at Tebra involves leading design and development of large software systems, focusing on payment facilitation platforms, with hands-on coding in Java, Spring Boot, and Python, and integrating third-party payment APIs. The position is remote within the United States but headquartered in Corona Del Mar, CA.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze information to determine, recommend, and provide innovative solutions to complex business problems related to existing system.
- Owner and subject matter expert for Large Software Systems and business initiatives.
- Lead Software Engineering Design Reviews to determine feasibility of design within time and cost constraints.
- Write software frameworks that can be extended by others on the team.
- Translate business requirements into software solutions.
- Optimize complex code in order to solve complex problems.
- Lead the architectural design and technical planning for major new platforms and features, such as the Worldpay Payment Facilitation system.
- Create technical specifications, select technologies, and define the end-to-end system architecture for mission-critical payment solutions.
- Hands-on development writing high-quality, production-level code for core payment systems using Java, Spring Boot, and Python.
- Build complex features, implement third-party API integrations like Stripe and TriPOS, and develop on cloud platforms like GCP and AWS.
- Improve team output and code quality, including performing detailed code reviews for peers, mentoring junior engineers on best practices in payment processing, and collaborating closely with product managers to translate business requirements into technical solutions.
- Serve as the subject matter expert and primary escalation point for production issues within the payments domain, which involves investigating and resolving complex payment discrepancies, managing system health on GCP, and writing code to patch critical issues.

Requirements (required):
- Master’s degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related field
- One (1) year of post-baccalaureate experience as a software engineer in the finance field or in a related position
- Experience with Java, Hibernate, Spring, Apache Struts
- Full-Stack Development
- Oracle database
- High-Volume Transaction Processing
- Data reporting and process automation

Requirements (preferred):
- none stated

Team and product context: The role involves leading design reviews and mentoring junior engineers, collaborating closely with product managers, and owning large software systems and business initiatives related to payments.

Work model, location, authorization: Position will be headquartered in Corona Del Mar, CA but is a telecommuting position, allowing for remote employment from various unanticipated worksites throughout the United States.
