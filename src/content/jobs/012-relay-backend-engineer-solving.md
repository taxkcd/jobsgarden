## The role

The Backend Engineer, Solving role at Relay involves building and owning the software that generates quotes, routes trades across on-chain and off-chain venues, and fills user requests end-to-end, focusing on reliability, pricing engines, and integrations with decentralized finance protocols.

Responsibilities:
- Design and iterate on the solver’s core quote-generation and pricing engine
- Build integrations with DEXs and aggregators
- Build observability and reliability tooling
- Collaborate with the Protocol team to integrate new settlement primitives

Requirements (required):
- Experience building systems that interact with on-chain protocols
- Strong TypeScript / Node.js backend engineering skills
- Understanding of DeFi primitives

Requirements (preferred):
- Experience with cross-chain bridging protocols
- Prior work on solver or market-making systems in a DeFi context
- Familiarity with Solana and Jupiter routing, or Bitcoin UTXO-based transactions
- Experience integrating with BullMQ, Redis-backed job queues, or similar async job infrastructure
- Track record shipping integrations with major DEX aggregators (1inch, LiFi, 0x, etc.)

Team and product context: The Solving team is responsible for Relay’s ability to execute requests with the best price and highest reliability, working closely with the Protocol team to integrate new settlement primitives.

Work model, location, authorization: Remote from: South America, North America, Europe, Africa, Asia, United States, Middle East, East Asia; Full time; English required.

Compensation: Competitive base salary; Equity package; Comprehensive health benefits; Unlimited PTO policy.
