## The role

The Developer Experience Engineer at Ritual will build foundational developer tools, frameworks, testing environments, and automation to enable seamless integration with Ritual's AI and blockchain platform. The role focuses on improving developer experience for AI-enabled blockchain applications, including CLI/SDK development, debugging tools, documentation, and community engagement.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement best-in-class development frameworks for AI-enabled chains
- Identify friction points in the developer journey and build solutions
- Build lightning-fast local development environments
- Create elegant CLIs and SDKs for AI and blockchain interactions
- Craft developer tooling for deploying and testing AI-enabled smart contracts
- Design debugging and simulation capabilities for AI-blockchain interactions
- Write clear documentation and tutorials
- Run workshops and hackathons to energize the developer community
- Build and nurture an active open-source community around development tools

Requirements (required):
- Strong experience with at least one of Rust, TypeScript, or Python
- Past experience contributing to open-source projects and libraries
- Proven ability to build developer tools that developers love to use
- Deep understanding of EVM architecture and smart contract development
- Experience with modern AI development workflows and MLOps tools
- Strong knowledge of container technologies and deployment patterns
- Proven ability to explain complex technical concepts through writing and speaking
- Ability to act quickly under high pressure and context switch frequently
- High level of end-to-end ownership and self-direction with excellent communication skills

Requirements (preferred):
- Track record of maintaining or significantly contributing to popular blockchain tools (e.g., Foundry, Anvil, wagmi, viem)
- Experience building compiler toolchains or language infrastructure
- Background in AI infrastructure and model deployment tooling
- Experience with build systems and package management
- Track record of growing developer communities through content and evangelism
- Deep knowledge of distributed systems and consensus mechanisms
- Experience designing intuitive APIs and developer interfaces

Team and product context: not stated

Work model, location, authorization: Fully remote and/or hybrid, up to you!

Compensation: Highly competitive compensation package, including annual discretionary bonus & optimized tax structure; top 1% of benefits across the web3 startup space; 100% of premiums covered on highest quality healthcare; aggressive company 401k match
