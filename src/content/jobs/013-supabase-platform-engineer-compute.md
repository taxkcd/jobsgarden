## The role

The Platform Engineer - Compute role at Supabase involves designing, building, and operating scalable, multi-region compute infrastructure that powers project provisioning, scaling, and compute services for users. The engineer will focus on automation, observability, cost optimization, and reliability of the compute platform.

Responsibilities:
- Help build and maintain the Supabase Cloud offering
- Design and implement systems to provision, scale, and manage the multi-region compute workloads powering Supabase projects
- Own the full lifecycle of compute orchestration: project creation, warm pools, autoscaling, resizing, and resource optimization
- Improve automation and observability across the compute stack to ensure high availability and performance
- Improve system architecture to reduce costs while balancing security, reliability, and performance
- Collaborate cross-functionally with other teams to help leverage compute as a core platform offering
- Participate in on-call rotations, incident response, and post-incident reviews
- Resolve escalated support issues and identify and resolve systemic issues across the platform

Requirements (required):
- Strong software engineering skills (Go and Typescript required)
- Experience building and operating cloud infrastructure or distributed systems at scale
- 5+ years experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS required, additional experience with GCP and Azure is a plus) - particularly compute, networking, and storage services
- 3+ years experience working with building custom Kubernetes controllers, and operating large-scale (tens-to-hundreds of thousands of pods) clusters
- Knowledgeable about networking and multi-region infrastructure design
- Familiar with Postgres
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools (Pulumi highly preferred, Terraform/CDK etc acceptable)
- Experience with QEMU, KVM, and NAS is a plus
- Experience with being part of on-call rotations for large production system

Requirements (preferred):
- none stated

Team and product context: The role is within the Compute team responsible for critical systems powering project provisioning, scaling, and compute services at Supabase. The company is fully remote with a globally distributed team of ~400 members across 60+ countries, emphasizing open-source-first and fast iteration.

Work model, location, authorization: Fully Remote
We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.
Flexible Work
We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time.

Compensation: ESOP (equity ownership), Tech Allowance, Health Benefits (100% for employees, 80% for dependents), Annual Off-Sites, Professional Development allowance
