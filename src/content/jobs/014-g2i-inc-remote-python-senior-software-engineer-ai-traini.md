## The role

This role is for a Senior Software Engineer to evaluate AI-generated coding interactions from models like OpenAI Codex, Claude Code, or Cursor, focusing on the quality, reasoning, and engineering judgment of AI outputs rather than writing production code.

Responsibilities:
- Evaluate AI-generated coding interactions end to end
- Judge whether outputs are useful, correct at a high level, and aligned with how a strong engineer would think
- Assess the quality of explanations and reasoning, not just the code
- Distinguish between levels of response quality (what makes something a 2 vs a 4)
- Give clear, opinionated feedback in writing on what worked, what didn't, and what felt off or misleading
- Help define what great looks like when working with tools like Cursor, Codex and Claude Code

Requirements (required):
- Senior, Staff or Principal-level engineer (or equivalent experience)
- Strong background in TypeScript/JavaScript or Python
- Hands-on experience with at least one of OpenAI Codex, Claude Code or Cursor
- Strong written and spoken English (B2 or above)
- Able to evaluate code without executing it or reviewing every line
- Comfortable giving direct, opinionated feedback
- High bar for what good engineering looks like

Requirements (preferred):
- Prior exposure to prompt design or evaluation workflows
- Experience mentoring senior engineers or defining engineering standards

Team and product context: Not explicitly stated; role is individual contributor focused on AI evaluation rather than traditional engineering.

Work model, location, authorization: Remote from anywhere; fixed term; 10-20 hours/week; $100-$200/hour depending on experience and location; eligibility may vary by location.

Compensation: $100-$200/hour depending on experience and location
