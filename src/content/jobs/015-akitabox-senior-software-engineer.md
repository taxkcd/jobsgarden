## The role

The Senior Software Engineer role at AkitaBox involves architecting and leading the development of scalable SaaS solutions, translating business needs into technical requirements, implementing secure and highly available web applications and APIs, and mentoring the team. The role emphasizes expert-level full stack proficiency (TypeScript, React, Node.js, NestJS, Prisma), AI-assisted development, and modern engineering fundamentals.

Responsibilities:
- Architect and lead the development of scalable solutions, making high-level technical decisions
- Translate business needs into robust technical requirements and epic plans
- Implement scalable, secure, low-latency and highly available web applications and APIs
- Design, build, debug, and maintain efficient, reusable, reliable, and readable code
- Execute full software development lifecycle
- Leverage AI-assisted coding tools to accelerate the software development life cycle while applying rigorous verification to ensure code quality and prevent 'AI slop'
- Follow industry standards in securing applications and data
- Perform validation and verification testing
- Write automated integration and unit tests
- Perform code reviews and offer constructive feedback
- Document and maintain software functionality
- Balance completed tasks vs implementing a perfect/elegant solution
- Participate in and help evolve agile processes and sprint planning to improve team velocity and quality
- Elevate the team with creative and critical thinking
- Mentor and guide the broader team to elevate collective technical capabilities

Requirements (required):
- 5+ years of experience in SaaS development, with a track record of successfully designing, building, and maintaining products in production
- Expert-level proficiency in the full stack (TypeScript, React, Node.js, NestJS, Prisma) and object-oriented design; openness to similar modern frameworks is welcome
- Proven ability in AI-assisted development, specifically managing AI harnesses, optimizing prompts, and maintaining code quality while detecting and remediating 'AI slop'
- Strong grasp of modern engineering fundamentals, including automated testing (unit/integration), CI/CD pipelines, Git/GitHub, and cloud infrastructure (AWS/containers)
- Self-driven and communicative professional with excellent creative problem-solving skills, adept at thriving in a fast-paced, collaborative team

Requirements (preferred):
- Experience designing, developing, deploying, observing, and maintaining native applications, especially with Flutter
- Experience with relational database and schema to third normal form
- Experience with analytical data pipelines and star schema, especially with dbt and Snowflake
- Experience with AWS infrastructure, especially with Terraform
- Solid understanding of UI/UX best practices and design systems (e.g., Material Design), with a strong eye for visual hierarchy and consistent spacing

Team and product context: The company is a startup with about 40 employees, values transparency, empathy, and personal and professional growth, and emphasizes a relaxed and flexible small business work environment with opportunities for growth and upward mobility.

Work model, location, authorization: Remote from: Anywhere; Full time

Compensation: Competitive Base; Medical, Dental and Vision Insurance; Unlimited Paid Time Off; Flexible Schedule
