## About

Software engineer from Islamabad, mostly frontend but comfortable across the stack, with a soft spot for design. Builds Meelio, a calm, local-first new-tab focus dashboard (timers, tasks, notes, ambient sounds; Chrome, Edge, Firefox and web), and deesign, which turns a written brief into pages, flows and slides and checks each design in a real browser. Ships many small tools on the side: Comment Sidecar (VS Code), Yap (on-device dictation for macOS), Orbit (AI agents building an orbital city), Lumidot (React loading animations), Scroll Sync and Offshelf (Chrome extensions).
